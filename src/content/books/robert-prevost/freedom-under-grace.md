Felt less like a cohesive collection of reflections on the Augustinian tradition and more like a quick collection of largely administrative and ceremonial talks. Some good insights to be found, but mostly my takeaway was that the burden of being a leader is saying more or less the same things, over and over, to various assemblies of your people.
