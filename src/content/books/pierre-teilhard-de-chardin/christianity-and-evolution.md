A collection of short essays from this futurist-thinking Catholic priest and scientist. While he repeats himself a bit, it's also interesting to see the evolution (no pun intended) of Teilhard's thought across the first half of the 20th century. Teilhard makes his clearest case here for aligning Christian thought about original sin and salvation with modern scientific understanding. Brilliant stuff.
